CHAPTER ONE

My Musical Journey

The strings may change. The joy never does.

I first picked up a guitar as a teenager, and music has stayed with me ever since. After decades of playing, I'm still learning, practicing, and discovering new things I want to try.

For most of my life, I played acoustic guitar. In recent years, I've been exploring the electric guitar, too. I own dozens of guitars, and there's always something special about picking one up and finding a new sound.

Among the musicians I've admired most are Bob Dylan, Simon & Garfunkel, and Mark Knopfler. I've enjoyed attending countless concerts over the years, and I still love getting together with fellow musicians for the occasional jam session at home.

A LEGACY IN SONG

Why I Created This Website

I wanted to create a place where my family, friends, and future generations could come back and enjoy the music I’ve played throughout my life. Ellen and I have shared more than 50 years together, and family has always been at the heart of my story. Each performance is a small piece of that story—a song preserved, a memory shared, and a reminder of the joy that music can bring.

More than anything, I hope these recordings make someone smile, remember a favorite song, or discover the pleasure of listening all over again.

Music brings people together.
